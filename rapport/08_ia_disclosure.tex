\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Use of Generative AI Tools}
This report was produced with the assistance of a generative AI
language model (Anthropic Claude), used as a support tool for
writing and coding tasks. AI assistance was limited to the
following:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Code assistance.}
  Iterative debugging and structural review of the Python
  pipeline (classifiers, cross-validation, preprocessing,
  and statistical tests).
  \item \textbf{Writing assistance.}
  LaTeX drafting, mathematical notation, and report
  structure refinement.
\end{itemize}
All analytical choices, model designs, interpretations, and
conclusions are the authors' own. All quantitative results
(accuracies, p-values, confusion matrices) were produced by
running the pipeline on the datasets of
Huang et al.~\cite{Huang2019} and verified by the authors.
The AI tool did not execute any code or produce numerical
results autonomously.
